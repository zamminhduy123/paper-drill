Research Splits Using Only: Car-Hacking, ROAD, CIC-IoV-2024, CAN-ML

Below are concrete research splits aligned with the thesis "application of deep learning methods into areas of In-Vehicle Network." Each split uses only the four named datasets and targets one or more of the likely limitations identified (lack of empirical validation, unclear real-hardware integration, limited scalability/attack coverage/defense effectiveness).

Split 1 — Intrusion Detection Generalization Across Attack Types

Datasets: Car-Hacking + ROAD

* Train: Car-Hacking (normal + known attack classes)

* Test (in-domain): Car-Hacking held-out split

* Test (cross-domain): ROAD (unseen attack families and driving conditions)

* Purpose: Measure whether a deep learning IDS trained on one vehicle-network attack corpus generalizes to another. Directly addresses limited attack coverage and lack of empirical validation .

* Metrics: Accuracy, macro-F1, false-negative rate per attack class, cross-dataset degradation Δ.

Split 2 — Scalability to Modern IoV Traffic

Datasets: CIC-IoV-2024 + Car-Hacking

* Train: CIC-IoV-2024 (large-scale IoV traffic)

* Validation: CIC-IoV-2024 held-out

* Test: Car-Hacking (legacy CAN attack data)

* Purpose: Assess whether models scaled on contemporary IoV data remain effective on classical in-vehicle CAN attacks. Addresses scalability and limited detail on scalability .

* Metrics: Throughput, latency, memory footprint, F1 on Car-Hacking, scalability curves vs. training size.

Split 3 — Cross-Dataset Transfer for CAN Signal-Level Detection

Datasets: CAN-ML + Car-Hacking

* Train: CAN-ML (signal-level CAN traces)

* Test: Car-Hacking (frame-level CAN attacks)

* Reverse transfer: Train on Car-Hacking, test on CAN-ML

* Purpose: Evaluate bidirectional transferability between signal-level and frame-level CAN representations. Addresses unclear integration with real vehicle hardware (signal-level data is closer to ECU/bus-level reality).

* Metrics: Bidirectional F1, domain-shift gap, per-signal detection latency.

Split 4 — Defense Effectiveness Under Realistic IoV Conditions

Datasets: CIC-IoV-2024 + ROAD + CAN-ML

* Train: CIC-IoV-2024 + ROAD (combined training)

* Validation: Held-out portions of both

* Test: CAN-ML (unseen in-vehicle signal data)

* Purpose: Test whether defenses learned from IoV-scale and road-level data transfer to in-vehicle CAN signals. Addresses defense effectiveness and attack coverage .

* Metrics: Detection rate, false-positive rate, robustness under noise, adversarial evasion rate.

Split 5 — Multi-Dataset Benchmark with Leave-One-Dataset-Out

Datasets: Car-Hacking, ROAD, CIC-IoV-2024, CAN-ML

* Folds:

+ Fold A: Train {ROAD, CIC-IoV-2024, CAN-ML} → Test Car-Hacking

+ Fold B: Train {Car-Hacking, CIC-IoV-2024, CAN-ML} → Test ROAD

+ Fold C: Train {Car-Hacking, ROAD, CAN-ML} → Test CIC-IoV-2024

+ Fold D: Train {Car-Hacking, ROAD, CIC-IoV-2024} → Test CAN-ML

* Purpose: Establish a reproducible leave-one-dataset-out benchmark. Addresses reproduction depends on external DOIs and README pipeline by defining a fixed, citable protocol.

* Metrics: Per-fold F1, average cross-dataset F1, variance across folds, reproducibility checklist.

Split 6 — Hardware-Proximity Validation Proxy

Datasets: CAN-ML + ROAD

* Train: CAN-ML (signal-level, closest to real CAN bus)

* Validation: ROAD subset (vehicle/road context)

* Test: ROAD held-out + CAN-ML held-out

* Purpose: Use signal-level and road-context data as a proxy for real vehicle hardware integration where physical testbeds are unavailable. Addresses unclear integration with real vehicle hardware .

* Metrics: Real-time inference latency, signal-level precision/recall, deployment feasibility score.

Split 7 — Temporal and Concept-Drift Robustness

Datasets: CIC-IoV-2024 + CAN-ML

* Train: Early temporal segment of CIC-IoV-2024 + CAN-ML

* Validation: Middle segment

* Test: Late temporal segment of CIC-IoV-2024 + CAN-ML

* Purpose: Evaluate degradation under temporal drift and evolving traffic. Addresses limited detail on scalability and attack coverage over time.

* Metrics: F1 decay over time, drift detection latency, retraining frequency needed.

Summary Table
