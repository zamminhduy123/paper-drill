Question

A memory-mapped, DMA-driven systolic-array accelerator attached to an unmodified RISC-V toolchain achieves the closed-form modelled latency and sustained throughput on real edge-AI inference workloads, not just a 4×4 matrix multiply.

Evidence

* supporting papers: [[Paper - FPGA validated RISC V system on chip with a custom systolic array accelerator fo]] reports that both Vivado XSim simulation and on-board runs confirm a 4×4 matrix multiply finishes in 320 ns at 100 MHz, giving a compute-phase throughput of 1.16 GOPS, with formal proof of two scheduling propositions plus cross-checking of a closed-form analytical performance model (support_passage).

* conflicting papers: not stated.

* unknown: Whether sustained throughput and power hold under continuous real inference workloads, and behaviour beyond the 4×4 tile size (limitation_unknown). Author-stated limitations: power, DMA-overlap, and sustained-throughput figures are estimates from Vivado post-implementation analysis and the analytical model rather than direct on-board measurement (limitation_author); no comparison against other RISC-V accelerator baselines on the same device, no sustained end-to-end throughput measurement under real workloads, and only a 4×4 matrix multiply is reported (limitation_inference). The extrapolation from 4×4 matrix multiply to real inference workloads is speculative.

Transfer

* Source field: FPGA edge-AI inference on a RISC-V SoC with a memory-mapped systolic-array accelerator (transfer_ivn).

* Mechanism: memory-mapped, DMA-driven accelerator with an unmodified toolchain and a closed-form latency/energy model (transfer_ivn).

* IVN problem: repurposed as an in-vehicle network edge inference node for real-time CAN or V2X traffic classification on low-cost FPGAs (transfer_ivn).

Experiment

* Data: in-vehicle CAN or V2X traffic classification streams on a Digilent Basys 3 board with a Xilinx Artix-7 XC7A35T at 100 MHz (eval_setting).

* Baseline: the reported 4×4 matrix multiply result of 320 ns and 1.16 GOPS at 100 MHz (support_passage).

* Shift-or-failure condition: irregular or streaming in-vehicle traffic patterns that fail to achieve the modelled latency and energy on the Artix-7 (transfer_risk).

* Comparison: modelled versus on-board sustained latency and throughput under real workloads, against the closed-form analytical model and XSim/on-board 4×4 numbers.

Value

* Measurement: detection delay.

* Job skill it improves: real-time in-vehicle network traffic classification on low-cost FPGAs.

* Feasibility: low-cost Artix-7 FPGA with an unmodified RISC-V toolchain; power, DMA-overlap, and sustained-throughput figures are estimates, and only 4×4 matrix multiply is validated, so on-board validation of streaming inference is required (limitation_author, limitation_inference).

* Novelty status: overlaps prior work
