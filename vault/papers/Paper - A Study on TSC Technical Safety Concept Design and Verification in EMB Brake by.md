# A Study on TSC (Technical Safety Concept) Design and Verification in EMB (Brake by Wire) Systems

## Novelty
The paper presents an ISO 26262-based Technical Safety Concept for an Electro-Mechanical Brake system, emphasizing redundancy, fault detection, fail-safe operation, and control handover under Main Center Control Unit and CAN communication faults.

## Methodology
The study develops the EMB safety concept across ISO 26262 Parts 3, 4, and 6, derives safety goals and functional safety requirements from HARA, and verifies the design using ECU-in-the-Loop Simulation, Hardware-in-the-Loop Simulation, and real-vehicle tests. Fault scenarios are injected with a custom LabVIEW-based fault injection tool to evaluate FTTI settings, error handling, and control transfer.

## Explicit Limitations
No explicit limitations are stated in the abstract.

## Future Work
Future work will focus on prognostics and maintenance strategies to support safe deployment of EMB systems in autonomous and electric vehicles.
