# Advanced IoT-Integrated Systems for Intelligent Water Quality Monitoring in Aquaculture: Emerging Technologies, AI-Driven Analytics, and Future Perspectives

## Novelty
Extends prior bibliometric and systematic IoT aquaculture analysis by reviewing 5G-enabled monitoring, AI digital twins, flexible nano sensors, federated learning, UAV/AUV platforms, and TinyML edge deployment.

## Methodology
Critical review and bibliometric/systematic analysis of 2020–2025 literature on IoT sensor networks, AI predictive models, edge computing, and aquaculture water quality monitoring.

## Explicit Limitations
Lack of automated TAN sensing in most commercial deployments, limited self-cleaning sensor maintenance mechanisms, and inequitable technology access in developing-nation aquaculture.

## Future Work
AI-digital twin co-simulation, bio-inspired nano sensor arrays, 5G-LPWAN hybrid architectures, and fully automated sustainable aquaculture ecosystems.
