Transfer IVN

Continual learning for in-vehicle network intrusion detection, adapting to new attack signatures without forgetting previously learned CAN bus attack patterns.
Transfer risk: IVN traffic is highly deterministic and protocol-specific (CAN, CAN FD), whereas 5G-IoT traffic is heterogeneous and service-based; continual-learning strategies tuned for non-IID 5G traffic may not capture the tight timing and physical-layer semantics of in-vehicle networks.
