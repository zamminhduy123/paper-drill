# Deep Learning for Big Data: A Survey of Architectures,Distributed Frameworks, and Emerging Challenges

## Novelty
The paper provides a survey-level synthesis of deep learning for big data, connecting neural architectures, Spark-based distributed training frameworks, and emerging deployment responses such as federated learning, explainable AI, and edge/TinyML for resource-constrained settings.

## Methodology
The work is a survey organized around three axes: neural architectures for large-scale data, distributed computing frameworks for tractable training, and persistent deployment challenges. It also reviews emerging responses and open research directions.

## Explicit Limitations
The abstract identifies scalability, data quality, privacy, and interpretability as persistent challenges that constrain real-world deployment. It also highlights gaps in communication-efficient distributed training, robustness to non-IID data, and standardized benchmarking.

## Future Work
Future directions include communication-efficient distributed training, improved robustness to non-IID data, and standardized benchmarking for big data deep learning systems.
