# EDGE-AI WITH EXPLAINABLE TINYML FOR REAL-TIME WATER QUALITY MONITORING AND PREDICTIVE ANALYTICS

## Novelty
Integrated TinyML, edge computing, and explainable AI for real-time water quality monitoring, predictive analytics, and early contamination warning on resource-constrained devices.

## Methodology
TinyML-based edge architecture deploys machine learning models directly on edge devices to process sensor data locally. XAI identifies influential water quality parameters such as pH, turbidity, and dissolved oxygen. The system monitors outliers and contamination, sends real-time warnings, and is evaluated for low latency, energy efficiency, and predictive accuracy against cloud-based methods.
