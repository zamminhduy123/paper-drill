# Edge AI for Real-Time Multimodal Quality Assessment

Novelty

The chapter surveys Edge AI for real-time multimodal quality assessment rather than proposing a single new model or algorithm. Its novelty lies in consolidating local processing of cameras, IoT sensors, environmental data, and product metadata into immediate quality decisions across agriculture, food processing, manufacturing, healthcare, pharmaceuticals, and environmental monitoring, while cataloging future enablers such as lightweight Vision Transformers, multimodal foundation models, federated learning, neuromorphic computing, energy-efficient accelerators, and adaptive model compression.

Methodology

Not stated. The text describes a chapter-level examination of Edge AI inferencing, factory machine vision, and edge-cloud collaboration, but no experimental design, datasets, training procedure, or evaluation protocol are provided.

Explicit Limitations

The chapter identifies challenges involving computational resources, energy consumption, synchronization, security, reliability, and model maintenance. These are stated as open issues for Edge AI in real-time multimodal quality assessment rather than limitations of a specific study.

Future Work

Future directions include lightweight Vision Transformers, multimodal foundation models, federated learning, neuromorphic computing, energy-efficient accelerators, and adaptive model compression.
