# Evaluation of Hybrid CAN XL–Ethernet Architectures for Next-Generation Automotive Networks

## Novelty
Comprehensive evaluation of hybrid CAN XL–Ethernet architectures for next-generation automotive networks, including CAN XL protocol features, compatibility with CAN FD, integration with automotive Ethernet, CANsec security, and representative zonal scenario comparison under mixed traffic and concurrent OTA updates.

## Methodology
Protocol and architecture analysis of CAN XL, CAN FD, and automotive Ethernet, followed by evaluation of representative zonal scenarios comparing CAN XL and CAN FD under mixed-traffic and concurrent over-the-air update conditions.
