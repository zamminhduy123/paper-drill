# Graph-CoLD: Graph-Diagnosed Collaborative Label Denoising for Network Intrusion Detection

## Novelty
Graph-CoLD proposes graph-diagnosed collaborative label denoising for noisy-label network intrusion detection, with evidence-preserving graph denoising and leakage auditing to determine when graph structure helps.

## Methodology
The abstract describes a reproducibility package containing source code, frozen results, paper-ready tables and figures, audit reports P1-P5, and manuscript source for the associated study.

## Explicit Limitations
Not stated explicitly; the abstract implies leakage risk and conditional usefulness of graph structure.
