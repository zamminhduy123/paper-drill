# PowerFlow-DNN: Compiler-Directed Fine-Grained Power Orchestration for End-to-End Edge AI Inference

Novelty

Compiler-directed, fine-grained power orchestration for end-to-end DNN inference on resource-constrained edge devices; power management decisions are made at compiler level and applied across the full inference pipeline rather than at coarse hardware or runtime granularity.
