# Protocol Weakness-Aware CNN-BiLSTM-Transformer Adaptive Fusion Network with Early-Risk and Uncertainty-Aware IoT Cyberattack Detection

## Novelty
Protocol-aware IoT intrusion detection framework combining CNN, BiLSTM, and Transformer branches with adaptive fusion and confidence-aware early-risk and uncertainty-based rejection.

## Methodology
PWAH-CBTAFNet learns spatial, temporal, and contextual traffic features, fuses them adaptively, and uses confidence-aware inference; evaluated on CICIoT2023 and ToN-IoT under within-dataset, cross-dataset, and robustness settings with accuracy, macro-F1, balanced accuracy, MCC, ROC-AUC, and PR-AUC.
