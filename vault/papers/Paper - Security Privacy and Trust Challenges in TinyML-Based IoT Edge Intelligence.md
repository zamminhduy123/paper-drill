# Security, Privacy, and Trust Challenges in TinyML-Based IoT Edge Intelligence

## Novelty
The chapter frames TinyML-based IoT edge intelligence as a security, privacy, and trust problem, reviews vulnerabilities such as adversarial attacks, model extraction, and data leakage, and proposes a framework for secure TinyML deployment.

## Methodology
The work is a review and framework proposal. It examines distributed TinyML deployment risks, surveys emerging countermeasures including federated learning, lightweight cryptography, blockchain integration, and hardware-based security, and synthesizes them into a secure deployment framework.
